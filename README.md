# 📰 OpenFront.io News Station Standards
> *Open. Transparent. Fair.*

These standards apply to **all news stations** posting on the OpenFront's Discord server. Our goal is to encourage high-quality, engaging journalism while maintaining fairness, community trust, and platform integrity.
## 📌 I. Core News Standards
### ✅ 1. Accuracy & Neutrality
* All articles must be **fact-checked** to the best of the author's ability.
* Serious news should aim for **neutral tone** and include multiple perspectives when covering controversial events. If covering multiple perspectives isn't possible, it should be explained why.
* Clearly label **opinions, satire, or clan-affiliated perspectives**.
> *Example: “This is an editorial and may contain subjective views.”*

### ❌ 2. No Malicious “Beefing”
* **Personal attacks, slander, false allegations**, and bad-faith provocations are prohibited.
* However, **respectful criticism and rebuttals** are allowed and encouraged, if factual and constructive.
> *Healthy debate is a part of journalism. Just don’t make it personal.*

### ❌ 3. No Harmful or Rulebreaking Content
* No racism, real-world politics, hate speech, or targeted harassment.
* Posts must comply with **server-wide rules** and moderation guidelines.

### ❌ 4. No Explicit Promotions
* No direct advertising of:
  * Other Discord servers
  * Social media pages
  * Clan recruitment or giveaways
* ✅ **Community news stations** may include **one masked link** at the footer of their article to redirect to their own archive or publication hub.
> ⚠️ *Clan-run stations are not allowed to include promotional links.*

### ✅ 5. Relevance to OpenFront
* Articles must relate to **OpenFront.io**, **clan politics**, **community events**, or **in-game events**.
* Out-of-scope or off-topic posts may be removed.

## 🛠️ II. Clan-Affiliated Station Policy
We recognize that **clan-backed stations** can contribute value; but transparency and fair conduct are required.
### ⚠️ 6. Disclosure Requirement
All stations **affiliated with a clan** must clearly state their affiliation somewhere on each article:
> `⚠️ This news station is affiliated with X Clan.`

### ✅ 7. Allowed with Conditions
Clan-run stations are **allowed** if they meet the following:
* Cover **non-clan news** regularly (not just propaganda)
* Avoid excessive promotion or glorification
* Submit to **the same quality expectations** as independent stations
## 🤣 III. Satire News
* Satire news are allowed, but they need to make it clear that they are satire (for example, with a small text at the footer).

## 🛡️ IV. Verification Requirements
To be considered "Verified", news stations must:
* Show **consistent formatting and effort**
* Demonstrate **responsiveness to mod feedback**
* Maintain a **non-toxic reputation**
## 🚫 V. Punishments & Strike System
Violations of the above standards will follow a **progressive, documented system**:
### 🟠 Minor Offenses
*(e.g., mild bias, unclear claims, low effort formatting)*
* 1st: Warning with feedback
* 2nd: 1–7 day posting cooldown
* 3rd: Demotion or probation
### 🔴 Major Offenses
*(e.g., misinformation, undisclosed clan propaganda, rulebreaking)*
* 1st: 2–7 day posting restriction
* 2nd: 1–2 week restriction + content review before posting
* 3rd: Permanent ban from news publishing privileges
### ⚫ Severe Offenses
*(e.g., racism, harassment, real-world hate, raid incitement)*
* Immediate removal of rights
* Potential full server punishment (timeouts, bans, etc.)
### ⚖️ Transparency & Appeal
* Affected stations may **appeal decisions** via tickets or DMs.
* Mod teams are encouraged to **communicate clearly** with stations before and after actions are taken.
## 🧠 Final Notes
* We don’t ban stations based on who runs them, we judge them by **how they act** and **what they produce**.
* These rules aim to foster a **competitive**, **creative**, and **trustworthy** news ecosystem in OpenFront.
